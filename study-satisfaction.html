Study Satisfaction in PE Teacher Education

Research project · University of Münster · Published 2026

Back to overview

Analysing survey data on study satisfaction among future physical education teachers.

R · Factorial ANOVA

As part of a mixed-methods research project at the University of Münster, I analysed online survey data from 553 PE teacher education students. Using R, I checked scale reliability with Cronbach’s alpha and used factorial ANOVAs to examine study satisfaction by gender, degree programme and school type. Significant interactions were followed up with comparisons of estimated marginal means.

I prepared the quantitative tables and wrote the quantitative methods and results sections. The study combined the survey analysis with qualitative interviews and was published in Beiträge zur Hochschulforschung in 2026.

Publication

Magner, A., Greif, A., & Kohake, K. (2026). Studienzufriedenheit im Sportlehramtsstudium: Eine Mixed-Methods-Studie unter besonderer Berücksichtigung des Geschlechts. Beiträge zur Hochschulforschung, 48(2), 110-139.
